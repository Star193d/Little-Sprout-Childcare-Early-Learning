Our policies in brief

Summaries only. Edit these to match your centre's official policies.

Drop-off and pick-up

Parents sign children in and out each day. Only people named on the registration form may collect a child, and we ask for photo ID if we do not recognise someone.

Illness and medication

Children who are unwell should stay home so they can rest and others stay healthy. If a child becomes ill at the centre, we call a parent right away. Medication is given only with written permission and clear instructions.

Allergies and meals

Please list every allergy and dietary need on the registration form. We keep this information visible to caregivers and review it with you whenever it changes.

Emergencies and first aid

We practise evacuation and lock-down routines and keep emergency contacts for every child on hand. In a medical emergency we call emergency services first, then you.

Hygiene and cleaning

Hand-washing is part of every routine: arrival, before meals, after toileting and after outdoor play. Nappy-change areas and shared surfaces are cleaned after each use.

Staff and supervision

All caregivers are screened before they start and supported with ongoing training. Children are supervised at all times, indoors and out.
